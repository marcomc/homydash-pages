A focused first release

The planned experience uses an iPhone camera or a connected Ray-Ban camera, subject to verified compatibility and authorised Meta availability. Microphone audio is optional where supported. iOS interruptions can pause or stop recording.

Automatic driving detection, GPS routes, app-managed iCloud backup and direct SSD copying are outside the first-release scope.
